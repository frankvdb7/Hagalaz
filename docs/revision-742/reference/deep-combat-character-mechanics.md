## 1. Accuracy, hit rolls and damage

\`CharacterCombat.GetMeleeDamage\`, \`GetRangedDamage\`, \`GetMagicDamage\`, and matching \`NpcCombat\` methods each compute their own effective levels/attack and defence rolls. The common source pattern for melee/ranged includes:

- Attack style adds **+3** for accurate or **+1** for controlled; target defensive style adds **+3** to defence or **+1** for controlled. Bonuses come from the current equipment/statistics and \`GetAttackBonusType\`.
- \`effectiveAttack = Round(attackLevel + 8 + attackBonus)\`; \`effectiveDefence = Round(defenceLevel + 8 + defenceBonus)\` (subject to style and prayer adjustments).
- \`attackRoll = Round(effectiveAttack * (1 + attackBonus/16) * 10)\` and \`defenceRoll = Round(effectiveDefence * (1 + defenceBonus/64) * 10)\`; character calculation then estimates \`accuracy = (attackRoll-1)/(2*defenceRoll)\` if lower, \`1-(defenceRoll+1)/(2*attackRoll)\` if higher and otherwise **0.5**, using a \`NextDouble()\` hit roll. Special bonuses adjust the rolls in weapon-specific paths. These are **current implementation expressions**, not an externally verified era formula.
- On a miss the calculator returns **-1**, not zero. \`CreatureCombat.PerformAttack\` captures target handles, calculates travel delay based on distance, calls the target's \`IncomingAttack\`, and schedules a later \`Attack\` against a *re-resolved handle*. Check that out-of-range or newly invalid targets are not accidentally hit through a lingering queued task.
- Damage rolls are passed through \`CreatureHelperTwo.PerformMeleeDamageCalculation\`, \`PerformRangedDamageCalculation\`, \`PerformMagicDamageCalculation\`; \`CharacterCombat.Attack\` and \`NpcCombat.Attack\` subsequently apply defence, absorption and HP. Never test accuracy by asserting only displayed hit splats: distinguish accuracy miss \`-1\`, protected hits, soaking, reflected hits and actual life-point subtraction.

Source melee max hit: \`13 + strengthLevel + strengthBonus/8 + strengthLevel*strengthBonus/64\`, then special/set modifier and truncate/floor per code. Player ranged: \`5 + (rangedLevel+8)*(rangedStrengthBonus+64)/64\` with optional bolt/set bonus. Magic max hit adds **3% for each currently boosted Magic level above experience-derived base**, then multiplies by \`1+magicDamageBonus/100\`. The NPC formulas are distinct (especially ranged), so do not assume symmetry.

**Important precision and formula test gates:** source rounds at several intermediate steps and uses \`int\` conversion, sometimes twice; use deterministic fixture vectors for zero/low/negative bonuses, equality of attack/defence rolls, boosted/drained levels, target defence style and extreme high rolls. Test both chance boundaries and outcomes with a controlled RNG. Verify whether equipment bonuses appear both inside effective attack *and* the later roll multiplier as intended; do not silently replace source equations with an external formula.

## 2. Weapon speeds and specials

\`EquipmentStore.LoadEquipmentDefinition\` reads default speed from \`ItemTypeLogic.GetAttackSpeed\`, a cache extra-param with fallback **4**. \`EquipmentScript.GetAttackSpeed\` returns the equipped definition's speed unless overridden. \`CharacterCombat.GetAttackSpeed\` resolves cast spell first, unarmed default **4**, otherwise the selected weapon script. Rapid ranged style subtracts **1**; a non-spell \`MiasmicSlowState\` then doubles the speed. Spell scripts supply \`GetCastingSpeed\` (the standard and several ancient spell classes currently return **5**). NPC \`GetAttackSpeed\` delegates to its active NPC script and the server NPC definition. **Weapon-specific ticks must be decoded from real index 19**; this dossier intentionally does not invent 742 item-specific speed values.

\`CharacterCombat.AttackTick\` checks special toggle, required energy and remaining stat energy; current source decrements energy **before** calling the equipment special handler and clears toggle. The code catches \`NotImplementedException\` and falls back to its unarmed path. Special scripts sometimes schedule multiple hits (dragon dagger has two; dragon claws manages a sequence, including custom hit splats); inspect their hit accounting, Soul Split, protection, XP and kill credit separately. The source cost catalog carries raw \`energy_units\`: the server uses **1000 units for a full bar**; do not label raw 500 as 500% or treat the attribute as a physical 742 cache ID verification. Verify no special energy is charged for an unsupported/failed invocation and that active state matches selected weapon after unequip/swap.

Tests: fast switching, locked/invalid target, stun/silence/disabled special, one-tick repeated input, energy boundary \`cost-1/cost/cost+1\`, cancelled projectile and vanished handle; weapon-override versus base fallback; exactly-once resource debit and hit/XP attribution; rapid-style speed floor; Miasmic effect restoration and logout.

## 3. NPC combat and player/NPC interaction

\`NpcCombat.GetAttackLevel/GetStrengthLevel/GetDefenceLevel/GetRangedLevel/GetMagicLevel\` use \`NpcStatistics\` with relevant bonuses. The script supplies attack style, type, range and speed, and may override \`PerformAttack\`, \`CanSetTarget\`, \`IsAggressiveTowards\`, poison, incoming attack and death hooks. Default \`NpcScriptBase\` aggression checks visible characters, distance \`attackRange+2\`, single-combat occupation and recent attackers; \`CombatAggressive\` also applies a combat-level differential condition. A boss or familiar can override this logic completely.

\`NpcCombat.OnDeath\` schedules an animation delay, kill attribution, \`OnKilledBy\` and either respawn or unregister; loot table is looked up through \`LootService\`. Source NPC ownership and [boss audit](../data/catalogs/npc-boss-script-audit.csv) are in [NPC gameplay dossier](deep-npc-gameplay.md). Confirm attacks cannot cross walls, invalid collision, instances, or line-of-sight; simple pathfinding and projectile checks are separate. NPC attack animation/graphics may come from DB overrides and individual scripts. **No game-wide NPC attack/speed/HP values can be verified without the populated database and cache.**

## 4. Prayer and curse mechanics

\`Prayers.ActivatePrayer(NormalPrayer/AncientCurses)\` guards prayer book, event permission (\`PrayerAllowEvent\`), level threshold, available prayer points and mutual exclusion; \`DeactivatePrayer\` removes modifiers. \`Prayers.Tick\` runs through active statuses, computes drain-rate scaled by equipment Prayer bonus using \`baseRate * (1 + prayerBonus/30)\` and adds **600 ms** progress per server tick. [50 source constants](../data/catalogs/combat-prayer-source-constants.csv) preserve *base milliseconds per prayer point* and level requirement, not effective drain or historical client UI indices. Cross-check against [historical prayer candidates](../data/researched/standard-prayers.csv) and [curses](../data/researched/ancient-curses.csv); keep different evidence classes separate.

Protection/deflection in \`CharacterCombat.IncomingAttack\` varies by \`DamageType.Full*\` vs \`Standard*\`. Source gives **full reduction** for corresponding Standard damage and a **0.6 multiplier** for protected Full damage; deflections schedule a reflected attack. Distinguish conventional prayers from curses, reflected damage, multiple independent protection buffs, shield, antifire and the curse animation/graphic callback. In particular the **ranged/magic/summoning conditional expressions lack parentheses around the full damage-type match** and must be characterized before claiming cross-style isolation. Verify that a ranged prayer never shields a magic/melee/dragonfire attack merely because it is active.

Quick prayers, prayer book switching, equipment bonuses, activation bitfields/configs, life-point thresholds (Redemption/Retribution), reflect recursion and PvP protection interactions all require explicit tests. Prayer book restrictions may be quest-gated outside \`Prayers\`; record those separately.

## 5. Combat magic, spells, autocast and rune ownership

\`SkillsMagicCombatDefinition\` database fields include \`ButtonId\`, \`BaseDamage\`, \`RequiredLevel\`, \`BaseExperience\`, two serialized rune arrays, cast/end/projectile graphic and animation IDs, and \`AutocastConfig\`. \`StandardCombatSpell\` consumes requirements through the character magic service; base casting speed **5**, attack distance **8**. The ancient smoke/shadow/blood/ice/miasmic families and other combat spells implement distinct variants, animations, impact timing, side effects, and area attacks. Follow \`CharacterCombat.GetCastedSpell\` (selected spell takes precedence over autocast), \`AttackTick\` (requirements checked and runes removed), and \`ICombatSpell.PerformAttack\`.

**Source verification finding:** \`StandardCombatSpell.PerformAttack\` first calls \`GetMagicMaxHit(victim, GetBaseDamage(caster))\` then passes that already boosted value as the \`baseDamage\` argument to \`GetMagicDamage(victim, maxDamage)\`, which calls \`GetMagicMaxHit(target, baseDamage)\` **again**. With boosted Magic/damage bonuses this can compound max-hit scaling. The calculated/advertised \`AttackParams.MaxDamage\` may diverge from actual sampled damage. Treat as a targeted correctness regression; don't automatically fix all spell implementations at once.

Test casting requirement enforcement on level/rune count/held staffs/inventory; single removal per cast; autocast activation after region transfer/relogin; cast interruption/invalid target/cast target movement, protected splashes, effects on successful hit only, multi-target attack cap and PvP permissions per target, magic XP credited for on-cast vs on-hit and delayed results.

## 6. Familiar combat, special moves and persistence

\`SummoningSkillService.SummonFamiliar\` resolves definition/scroll, validates levels and resources, selects script, spawns from \`INpcBuilder\`, consumes the pouch and changes Summoning level. \`FamiliarScriptBase.AttachToSummoner\` owns summoner event handlers, death/destroy/dismiss cleanup and initial special points (**60**). The familiar is nonaggressive by default, rejects its summoner as target and attack source, cannot ordinarily be suspended, and follows or teleports to the summoner. \`CheckPerformSpecialMove\` checks special points and an inventory scroll; \`PerformSpecialMove\` removes the scroll and drains required points. \`Tick\` decrements lifetime, restores special points (source: **15 per 50 ticks**, cap **60**) and drains Summoning skill every 100 ticks.

\`FamiliarCharacterScript\`, \`FamiliarHydrator\`, \`FamiliarDehydrator\`, \`CharactersFamiliar\` and \`FamiliarInventoryContainer\` own restoration of familiar lifespan, special status and carried items. This is separate from index-18 NPC definitions and NPC persistent spawns. Verify disconnection/death/dismiss/cancellation in every ordering, no orphan NPC or duplicated inventory; restore summoner and familiar in a permitted zone, no stale handles; duel summoning ban must include already-existing familiars and familiar-initiated attacks. The 742 familiar cost/level/scroll IDs require real summoning DB data.

## 7. Death, skull, item conservation and PvP

\`CharacterCombat.OnDeath\` marks dead and schedules killer resolution, \`OnKilledBy\` and \`Owner.Respawn()\` after death animation. \`OnKilledBy\` consults \`CanBeLootedBy\`, \`GetItemsOnDeathData\`, inventory and equipment, creates ground items with the correct owner and restores kept items. The source drops item ID **526** for the generic bones record; this is current Hagalaz code, not a verified universal player-death rule. \`CharacterCombat.CheckSkullConditions\` may skull on attack initiation against a character or familiar, with source **2000** duration units. Confirm the killer-less or disallowed-loot death path still has a well-defined inventory/respawn outcome, and that item conservation is maintained when inventory, equipment, banking or trading are concurrent.

\`Wilderness.CanAttack\` checks \`Math.Min(GetWildernessLevel(attacker),GetWildernessLevel(target))\` against absolute combat-level difference using base or full level depending on familiar; NPC familiar has its own branch. \`GetWildernessLevel\` uses separate Y-coordinate formulas above **9900** and in surface regions. Standard teleports are blocked if level **>20**; item teleport override currently only delegates to base with an inline TODO for higher restrictions. The exact region boundaries and zone rules are **not** encoded by this one formula: review area registry and map collision. \`DuelArenaCombatScript\` denies external attackers, prevents ordinary player loot and skull rendering, and delegates restrictions to \`DuelRules\` and registered events. Duel rules cover disabled attack styles, food/drinks, prayers, specials, equipment slots, movement and summoning. Do not conflate duel deaths with Wilderness ground loot or normal respawns.

Test level difference at boundary \`L-1,L,L+1\`, both in/out of Wilderness and multi-combat, character vs familiar both ways, temporary familiar summon and dismissal, non-player defenders, teleport thresholds, forced movement/teleblock, safe areas and duel acceptance; test death while trading/dueling, while skulled/unskulled, Protect Item prayer, destroy-on-death gear, looting ownership and restart persistence. The server, not the client menu, is the last authorization authority.

## 8. Audit findings and completed verification boundary

See [source-backed findings](../data/catalogs/combat-mechanics-review-findings.csv) and [verification matrix](combat-verification-matrix.md). Priority findings are: cross-style protection-prayer operator precedence; special energy debited before unsupported handler/fallback; standard combat magic applying max-hit adjustment twice; failure/scope checks on death and pending projectile; speed/accuracy parity questions; familiar lifecycle and PvP gate symmetry.

These are *source-audited* findings, not proof that every path is hit in production, and are not code changes. Completion requires tests using the existing .NET test projects, seeded random inputs, approved index-18/19 source fixture and read-only DB export, client button/packet captures and one player-vs-NPC plus one PvP end-to-end capture per representative rule. Do not mark P1 #9 "verified complete" until those gates pass. No speculative refactor or new attack calculation subsystem is recommended.

## 9. Ancient spell source variant table

[20 concrete ancient spell source variants](../data/catalogs/combat-ancient-spell-script-rules.csv) were extracted from `AncientBloodCombatSpell`, `AncientIceCombatSpell`, `AncientMiasmicCombatSpell`, `AncientShadowCombatSpell` and `AncientSmokeCombatSpell`. Each family has exactly four source-ordered `variant_index=0..3` entries with requirement level, base magic XP/damage, autocast config and ordered rune/count pairs. **Do not sort these IDs or rename them to client buttons without a source-backed mapping.** Miasmic source base damage is initialized in its static constructor, whereas the other four use field initializers. The table is source-verified, **not** DB-backed, client-confirmed, or evidence that all five families are enabled in the current world.
